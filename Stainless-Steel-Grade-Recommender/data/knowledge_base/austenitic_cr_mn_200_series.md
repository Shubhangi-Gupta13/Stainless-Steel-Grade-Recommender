# Austenitic Cr-Mn (200-Series) Stainless Steels - Technical Reference & Jindal Special Grades

## Metallurgical Concept: Replacing Nickel with Manganese & Nitrogen
The 200-series stainless steels are chromium-manganese-nickel austenitic alloys. Because Nickel is an expensive and volatile alloying element, manganese (5% to 11%) and nitrogen (0.10% to 0.30%) are used as potent austenite stabilizers:
- **Austenite Phase Stabilization**: Nitrogen is ~30× more effective than nickel as an austenite former on an atom-for-atom basis.
- **Solid Solution Strengthening**: Interstitial nitrogen significantly increases yield strength (310–350 MPa compared to 205 MPa for conventional 304) while maintaining elongation >40%.
- **Cost Reduction**: Substantially lowers raw material cost and volatility compared to 300 series.

## Role of Copper Addition (J204Cu, JSL AUS, J4, JSL U DD)
Conventional 200-series grades (like 201) have high work-hardening rates, requiring frequent interstage annealing during severe drawing.
- **Jindal Stainless Innovation**: Controlled addition of 1.5% to 4.0% Copper ($Cu$).
- Copper lowers the stacking fault energy (SFE), suppressing deformation-induced martensite during cold forming.
- Greatly enhances drawability and stretch-formability: Limit Drawing Ratio (LDR) reaches 2.0 to 2.15, Erichsen cupping 12.8–13.5 mm.
- Enhances resistance to stress corrosion cracking (SCC) and corrosion in dilute sulfuric and organic acids.

## J216L: High-Performance Austenitic Substitute for 316L
J216L is a Chrome-Nickel-Molybdenum austenitic steel with manganese, nitrogen, and copper:
- **Mechanical Strength**: Annealed Yield Strength $\ge 310$ MPa (50% higher than 316L's 170–205 MPa).
- **Corrosion Resistance (MARC & PREN)**:
  - $\text{PREN} = 25.02$ (compared to 23.53 for 316L)
  - $\text{MARC} = 21.57$ (compared to 21.08 for 316L)
  $$\text{MARC} = \%Cr + 3.3\%Mo + 20\%C + 20\%N - 0.5\%Mn - 0.25\%Ni$$
- **Boiling Acid Tests**: In boiling 5% Citric Acid, 5% Tartaric Acid, 5% Acetic Acid, 2% HCl, and 65% $HNO_3$, J216L demonstrated lower corrosion rate (mmpy) and superior passivation compared to standard 316L.
- **Electrochemical Pitting**: In 3.5% NaCl at 23°C, pitting potential $E_{pit} = 335$ mV, comparable to 316L (372 mV).
- **Non-Magnetic Properties**: Retains very low magnetic permeability even after cold working.

## Food Safety & Chemical Leaching Studies (Atomic Absorption Spectrometry)
Extensive testing by Jindal Stainless of J204Cu and JSL AUS in food simulants and real food media (boiled for 30 minutes in cookware):
- **Tested Media**: 3% Acetic acid (pH 2.59), 3% Citric acid (pH 2.82), 3% Lactic acid (pH 2.80), 1% Tartaric acid (pH 2.27), 10% Ethanol (pH 5.82), 4% Common salt (pH 6.1), Tomato soup (pH 3.6).
- **Results**: Chromium and Nickel leaching into food was strictly $<0.1$ ppm (well within international food safety thresholds). No pitting or surface staining observed.

## Application Matrix
- **JSL U DD / J4**: Deep-drawn kitchenware up to 10 inches deep, bins, cookware.
- **J204Cu**: Domestic and commercial refrigerator doors, washing machines, kitchenware, handrails, automotive hose clamps.
- **J201 / J202**: Structural members for railway coaches, bus frames, indoor architectural trims.
- **J216L**: Marine architectural hardware, chemical vessels, food processing equipment, road tankers.
